A lively, disinterested, persistent liking for truth is extraordinarily rare. Action and faith enslave thought, both of them in order not to be troubled or inconvenienced by reflection, criticism or doubt. 
Every life is a profession of faith and exercises an inevitable and silent influence. 
The smallest seed of faith is better than the largest fruit of happiness. 
Faith is the bridge supporting what we see unto the scene that we do not. 
Faith is not going to want to know what is true. 
It's faith in something and enthusiasm for something that makes a life worth living. 
What is faith but a kind of betting or speculation after all? It should be, "I bet that my Redeemer live." 
Faith is the bird that feels the light and sings when the dawn is still dark. 
I have great faith in fools--self-confidence my friends call it.
