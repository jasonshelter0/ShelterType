Remember 
I remember when we once laughed, 
lying beneath the stars. 
We talked of deep and personal thoughts, 
Ones no one else had known. 
I remember our long embraces, 
and how I fit so perfectly in your arms. 
I know your eyes can see the real me, 
and also your perfect face. 
I remember your sweet hands; 
they defended and protected me. 
How they ran through my hair, 
while you told me how important I am to you. 
I remember how we could stare into each other's eyes, 
And how we thought we could read each other's minds. 
I remember how you hurt me. 
You left me standing alone. 
As I watched you walk away, 
I felt a tear roll down. 
I remember all those nights, 
crying myself to sleep, 
knowing you're not here, 
and wondering where you are. 
I remember.
