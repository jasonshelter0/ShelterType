Love revisited 
As the distant shadow of the setting sun drifts across the field. 
It reminds me of how your feelings changed, and made my world stand still. 
And the awesome wonder of Dusk's red haze, takes me back to a special place. 
Where I could feel the warmth of your hand on my face. 
I could smell your scent as the wind blew by, I knew you were there and I knew you were mine.
And the taste of your lips still lingered so long. 
And the sound of your voice, the beauty of your song. 
That was a time not long ago, when our hearts were one, joined with body and soul.
Though it is gone, like this dawn I see, we can bring it back, just you and me.
At the end of the day, when dusk is anew, you'll know I am there, and I'll know it is you.
